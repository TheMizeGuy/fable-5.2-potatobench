# Balance tournament: latest run

Generated by `npm run balance` (tools/balance/run.ts). Do not edit by hand; rerun instead.

## Setup

- 200 farms, each with seasons of its own made from its seed as the shipped game makes them (src/sim/climate.ts), every bot on every farm, 10-season campaigns from Year 1: 13000 campaigns. A smaller run plays the main bots over the record's 43 real start years (1973 to 2020), 6 seeds, five seasons: 774 campaigns.
- Every bot reads the game only as a player can: the functions in `src/sim/view.ts` and `src/sim/advice.ts`, the market quote, the public campaign fields and the events the game reports. `tests/bots.test.ts` checks the bot source for the names of hidden state.
- Net worth is cash plus equipment at half its price and land the farm bought at its price, less what is owed on the land mortgage. A farm is kept when it reaches the end of the campaign without foreclosure; "kept 5 yr" is the game's own goal, five seasons.
- Intervals are 95% bootstrap intervals over farms (each seed is its own weather and prices), 2000 resamples.

Run time 929 s on 14 worker threads.
