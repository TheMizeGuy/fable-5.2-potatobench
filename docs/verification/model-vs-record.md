# The model against the outside record

Generated by `npm run validate` (tools/validate/run.ts). Every number below comes from that run; rerun it after any change to `src/sim`. The comparisons are reported whatever they show; nothing in the model was tuned toward them.

## Setup

- Each playable year (51: 1973 to 2024, without 1996) is played as a single season from the opening farm, 8 seeds each (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8), averaged.
- **Reference practice** is the in-game advice followed as given (`reference` bot in tools/balance/bots.ts): the default plan (four fields of certified Katahdin, the two worn fields resting), planting, two hillings, weekly scouting, spraying on the blight watch, two test digs, vine kill and digging when the advice says. The reference buys no equipment, and without an irrigation reel the advice waters only a field that is wilting (below 20% of available water), so reference practice is in effect dryland, as most Aroostook potatoes are. The irrigated comparison is reported beside it.
- **Marketable yield** is cwt per potato acre graded saleable at the dig (US No. 1, chef, B, No. 2 and thin-skinned), before storage shrink. The USDA figure is production over harvested acres for the whole state (src/data/records.json).
- **Unsprayed** is the same practice with no fungicide, no roguing and no vine kill, so the epidemic runs until the vines die. **Peak blight** is the season peak of the true blighted share of leaf on the worst field, read from the year-end ledger.
- **Dryland** skips all watering, even of a wilting field; **irrigated** is reference practice that also waters whenever a bulking field falls below 60% of available water (the UMaine pivot trigger is 55-65%).

## Late blight

Season severity values are the model's Blitecast total from May 25 to freeze-up. The weather report's total (00-weather-data, June 1 to September 15, the same wet-hour data) is beside it. MacKenzie (1981) found 81% of Pennsylvania station-seasons below 90 and 42% below 60; at Caribou in the model, 88% of seasons are below 90 and 31% below 60.

A season is flagged an **epidemic** when the unsprayed peak averages 50% of leaf or more. The model flags 43 of 51 seasons: 1973, 1974, 1975, 1976, 1977, 1978, 1979, 1980, 1981, 1983, 1984, 1985, 1986, 1987, 1988, 1990, 1991, 1992, 1993, 1994, 1997, 1998, 1999, 2000, 2001, 2002, 2003, 2004, 2005, 2008, 2009, 2010, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2014, 2015, 2016, 2019, 2021, 2022, 2023, 2024.
