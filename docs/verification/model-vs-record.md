# The model against the outside record

Generated by `npm run validate` (tools/validate/run.ts). Every number below comes from that run; rerun it after any change to `src/sim`. The comparisons are reported whatever they show; nothing in the model was tuned toward them.

## Setup

- Each playable year (51: 1973 to 2024, without 1996) is played as a single season from the opening farm, 8 seeds each (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8), averaged.
- **Reference practice** is the in-game advice followed as given (`reference` bot in tools/balance/bots.ts): the default plan (four fields of certified Katahdin, the two worn fields resting), planting, two hillings, weekly scouting, spraying on the blight watch, watering below 38% of available water, two test digs, vine kill and digging when the advice says.
- **Marketable yield** is cwt per potato acre graded saleable at the dig (US No. 1, chef, B, No. 2 and thin-skinned), before storage shrink. The USDA figure is production over harvested acres for the whole state (src/data/records.json).
- **Unsprayed** is the same practice with no fungicide, no roguing and no vine kill, so the epidemic runs until the vines die. **Peak blight** is the season peak of the true blighted share of leaf on the worst field, read from the year-end ledger.
- **Dryland** skips all watering; **irrigated** waters whenever a bulking field falls below 60% of available water (the UMaine pivot trigger is 55-65%).

## Late blight

Season severity values are the model's Blitecast total from May 25 to freeze-up. The weather report's total (00-weather-data, June 1 to September 15, the same wet-hour data) is beside it. MacKenzie (1981) found 81% of Pennsylvania station-seasons below 90 and 42% below 60; at Caribou in the model, 86% of seasons are below 90 and 35% below 60.

A season is flagged an **epidemic** when the unsprayed peak averages 50% of leaf or more. The model flags 33 of 51 seasons: 1973, 1974, 1975, 1976, 1977, 1978, 1979, 1980, 1981, 1984, 1985, 1986, 1987, 1988, 1992, 1993, 1994, 1997, 1998, 2001, 2003, 2004, 2008, 2009, 2011, 2012, 2013, 2015, 2016, 2019, 2022, 2023, 2024.
