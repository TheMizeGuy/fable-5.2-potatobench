# The County: how the game talks

The owner, after playing: "can you fix the weird writing prose throughout the game such as 'At each year's end the
bank and the household take $19,000. The crop has to pay it. Your crew does the field work. You make the calls. I
stop the clock and say why each time.' it just sounds off".

It sounds off because the game was written in a clipped, slightly antique manner: no contractions, short flat
declaratives stacked one after another, odd constructions in place of ordinary ones ("sees to it by itself", "the
days go on", "puts it away", "wants an answer", "is held"), colons and semicolons doing the work of sentences. It
reads like a translation. This file is the rule for every word a player reads.

## The voice

A friendly, plain-spoken person who knows farming and is glad to explain it: the county agent leaning on the fence.
Modern everyday American English. The period lives in the things (a telegram, a weigh ticket, the ledger), never in
the grammar.

1. **Talk the way people talk.** Use contractions (you'll, it's, don't, I'll, that's, can't). Say "you" and "your".
   Use ordinary words in their ordinary order.
2. **Whole, connected sentences.** Join related thoughts with "so", "because", "but", "and", "which". Do not stack
   three five-word sentences. A fragment is fine only as a label or a button.
3. **Say why with "so" or "because".** "Blight can wipe out a field in one wet week, so spray before the rain."
4. **Use the words games use.** Pause, resume, speed, click, tap, select, order, tutorial, got it, skip. The game is
   "paused", not "held" and not "the clock stands". Time "passes" or "runs"; days do not "go on".
5. **No farm word without its meaning the first time.** "Hilling (piling soil over the row)".
6. **Short still.** Natural is not longer. Every length limit in the tests stays: a tutorial note is 35 words at
   most, the season screen stays under its word budgets. If the natural sentence is longer, cut an idea, not the
   grammar.
7. **Buttons are verbs a player would say.** "Got it", "Start the season", "Skip ahead", "Turn off the tutorial",
   "Sell now", "Store it".
8. **Errors and refusals say what to do next.** "Pick a field first: click one, or press its number key."
9. Punctuation: periods and commas. A colon only before a list or a value. No semicolons in player-facing text. No
   exclamation marks. No emoji. Numbers as figures with units ("$19,000", "4 fields", "about a week").
10. Screen-reader text follows the same voice.

## Words to change everywhere

| Instead of | Write |
| --- | --- |
| Clock held / the clock is held / stops the clock | Paused / the game is paused / pauses the game |
| the days go on / the days run on | time moves on / the game resumes |
| Noted | Got it |
| Stop the guide | Turn off the tutorial |
| the guide / the guided first year (as a name) | the tutorial / Guided first year (keep as the menu's name) |
| sees to it by itself / does it by itself | handles it automatically / does it on its own |
| Nothing for you to do. | You don't need to do anything. |
| a call / your call / the calls | a decision / your decision / the big decisions |
| wants an answer | needs an answer |
| put it away / puts away | close it / dismiss |
| is on it | is working on it |
| takes it / will take it | will do it |
| the list / on the list | the work list / on the work list |
| the note (the bank's) | the bank payment (say once: "the yearly bank payment") |
| the household / the household's keep | your family's living costs |
| at the year's end / at each year's end | at the end of the year / at the end of each year |
| the dig | the harvest (the order stays "Dig") |
| cwt | cwt (keep it), explained once per screen as "hundredweight (100 lb)" |
| It is / do not / cannot / will not / you have | It's / don't / can't / won't / you've |

Keep as they are: field and people names, "crew", "order", "stamp", "telegram", "weigh ticket", "ledger", "almanac",
the job names (Plant, Hill, Scout, Spray, Water, Rogue, Test dig, Kill vines, Dig, Haul), the speeds (1×, 3×, 8×).

## Before and after

| Before | After |
| --- | --- |
| At each year's end the bank and the household take $19,000. The crop has to pay it. | At the end of each year you owe $19,000 to the bank and your family, so your crop needs to earn at least that. |
| Your crew does the field work. You make the calls. I stop the clock and say why each time. | Your crew handles the field work and you make the big decisions. I'll pause the game and explain each one as it comes up. |
| These tags are your fields: 4 in potatoes, 2 resting. Nothing is planted yet: the soil is too cold. | These tags mark your fields. Four will grow potatoes and two are resting this year. It's still too cold to plant. |
| Planting has begun, with no order from you. | Your crew has started planting. You didn't need to tell them. |
| The crew does it by itself on September 15. A stamp only does it sooner. | Your crew will do this automatically on September 15. Click a stamp if you'd rather do it sooner. |
| Nothing for you to do until blight weather comes: that is your first call. | You don't need to do anything until blight weather arrives. That'll be your first big decision. |
| A telegram wants an answer. Answer it, or put it away (Escape) to go on. | This telegram needs an answer. Reply, or press Escape to close it and keep playing. |
| Select a field first: click it, or press its number. | Pick a field first. Click one, or press its number key. |
| There is blight on the farm. Protectant only works if it is on the leaf before the spores. | Blight is on your farm. Spray only protects leaves it reaches before the blight does, so don't wait. |
| The plants are a hand high. Soil pulled up now covers the tubers to come and buries the weeds. | The plants are a hand tall. Hilling now covers the potatoes that are about to form and buries the weeds. |
| Kills the vines. The tubers stop growing and their skins start to set. | Kills the vines, which stops the potatoes growing and lets their skins toughen for harvest. |
| The farm in this browser is erased and cannot be brought back. Save a copy first if you might want it. | This deletes the farm saved in this browser, and it can't be undone. Save a copy first if you might want it back. |
| The game saves itself in this browser. | The game saves automatically in this browser. |
| The book keeps the cash at each year end, not month by month. | The ledger shows your cash at the end of each year, not month by month. |
| Rot has run through the Katahdin from North Ridge. More than half the pile is gone. | Rot has spread through the Katahdin from North Ridge, and more than half of it is gone. |
| Not enough cash for the contractor: $6,000. | You can't afford the contractor yet. It costs $6,000. |
| Nothing needs doing here now. | Nothing needs doing here right now. |
| You take it once the digging in North Ridge is done, in about 2 days. | You'll get to this in about 2 days, once you've finished digging North Ridge. |
| Sprayed on Jun 7, and that has worn thin. Each order is one pass: spraying waits for your order. | You last sprayed on June 7 and it's wearing off. Each order is a single spray, so order another. |

## What must not change

Meaning, numbers, rules, and what any control does. Test ids, `data-k` keys, key bindings, aria roles. The length
limits the tests hold. Where a test asserts on words, change the test to the new words in the same commit and say so.
