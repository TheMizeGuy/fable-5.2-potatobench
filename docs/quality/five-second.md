# The five-second test

14 November. A still of the game is shown to a viewer who knows nothing about it and asked: what is the game asking
of you now; what would you press first; how would you build something or get an upgrade; what is your goal and
where did you learn it; what can you not read; what is too big, misplaced or empty. Each gives a mark out of ten for
"I know what to do next". The viewers are fresh agents with one of two outlooks (a player of strategy and colony
games who reads no small print; a player of casual farm games who skims), given only the pictures.

It is a check of the first seconds, on stills: it says nothing of how the game plays over an hour.

## Before (`live-54`)

Pictures: the first screen after Play, and the owner's own screenshot a few minutes in.

| | first screen | a few minutes in | "how would you build or upgrade?" | "what is your goal?" |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| strategy player | 7 | 5 | "No visible way" | "Unclear ... nothing says that outright" |
| casual player | 7 | 4 | "I see no way to build or upgrade" | "The screen never says" |

Both, of the second picture: the money board "a big blank white box"; "Order it: does this buy something or give a
command?"; the field labels "float away from their fields"; "Costs go on the loan until the crop sells: which
costs?".

## After (this round, first pass)

Pictures: the first screen after Play (with tips), a July farm with two people idle, and the build menu.

| | first screen | later | "how would you build or upgrade?" | "what is your goal?" | build menu |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| strategy player | 8 | 6 | "The BUILD button at the bottom right" | "Keep my money above $0 for 5 years" | 6 |
| casual player | 7 | 5 | "The BUILD button at the bottom right" | "don't go broke for 5 years" | 6 |

What they still marked down, and what was done about it in the same round:

- "Three prompts stack down the left side with no clear first one": the slip for idle people is now the strong one
  (canary, with the fist) and says who is idle in bold; "Give them work:" is no longer printed.
- "Killing the vines sounds like a disaster": its line now says what it is for ("to toughen the skins for digging").
- "Fell is jargon": the key says "Cut trees".
- Build menu, "nothing says whether a purchase would push my year-end forecast toward the goal" and "no recommended
  pick": each thing that can be had says what it leaves of the year-end forecast; the farm stand is marked as a good
  first building; four keys at the top go to the sheet's four parts.
- Not done: every potato field's stamp as a labelled Plant key (one is; four crowded a small window); the tip and
  the slip saying the same thing at the first screen; words of the trade in the build menu (cwt, crew-days).
