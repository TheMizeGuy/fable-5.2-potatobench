From the almanac

How to play

The crop grows where you cannot see it. Give the orders, let the days run, and dig day tells the truth.

1Pick a field

Click its tag, or the field. Its card opens at the right with what it needs.

2Give the order

Click the stamp, or right-click the field: it gives the order the field needs.

1×2×3×

3Let the days run

The days run, and stop when something needs you. Space pauses.

Red: blight or a loss. Act now.

Ochre: the next thing to do.

Blue: the field is drying out.

Black: work that is due.

SpringPlan the fields, hire, rent if you like.
The seasonPlant, hill, scout, spray, dig.
Dig dayThe weigh ticket: deliver, sell or store.
WinterSell from the cellar; fell and build.
Year endThe bank wants its note.
