8The code each mark means one thing, everywhere

A stamp on the land: something needs you, inked by kind. Red, blight or loss; ochre, the suggested next; blue, water; black, work due. Always its cut.
Pencil: what you did or chose: a tick, an order given, a struck goal, the best way ringed.
The printer’s fist: the one next thing: the goal, the suggested order, what brings most.
Canary: the goal slip, and only that.
